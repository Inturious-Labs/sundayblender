**Herbert Yang** is the editor of [The Sunday Blender](/about/). A writer, developer, and investor in AI and Web3, Herbert is a classically trained finance executive who also ships production code. He operates at the intersection of institutional finance, enterprise technology, and digital assets, with 25+ years of cross-border experience across the US, China, Singapore, Japan, and Europe. He is currently the founder of [Inturious Labs](https://inturious.com/), an AI-native development studio building efficient workflows for content creators and solo investors.

Early issues were signed with his pen name, [Clayton Man](https://weekly.sundayblender.com/p/we-come-this-far-now-what/).

His editorial team consists of:

- **claude.ai** for narration
- **Grok Bot** for fact checking
- **Pi** and **Claude Code** for site building

With The Sunday Blender, Herbert has created a one-person publishing workflow that any aspiring content creator could replicate, as he explained in his talk "[The One Person Publishing House](https://inturious.com/decks/one-person-publication-stack/)".

To find out more about Herbert, please visit his [personal site](https://herbertyang.xyz/docs/about/herbert-yang/) and reach out via email `herbert@inturious.com`.
